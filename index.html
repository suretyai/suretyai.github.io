The problem

  1. 01

    Moderation volume grows faster than your team.

  2. 02

    Models go stale the moment language shifts.

  3. 03

    Every uncertain call ends in a guess or a queue.

The loop is the product.

A frozen model does step one and stops. Fronesa runs all five, every week.

  1. 01

    Decide

    Every call returns a label and a confidence score, plus a receipt.

  2. 02

    Escalate

    Low-confidence calls go to a human reviewer instead of a guess: no model is right every time, so every decision carries its receipt.

  3. 03

    Learn

    Reviewer labels are logged as training data. Nothing is thrown away.

  4. 04

    Retrain

    The model retrains on the accumulated labels each week.

  5. 05

    Gate

    A new version ships only if it beats the current one on held-out data.

Straight answers

Does my data train other customers’ models?
Your labels train only your model — unless you explicitly agree otherwise. Agreement §5.6
Will this replace my reviewers?
No. The loop runs on their labels — their corrections are what teach the model.
How much work is the integration?
One endpoint. If you can send JSON, you’re integrated.
