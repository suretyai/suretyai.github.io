Try the model.

Tap any message to explore genuine pre-computed outputs from the FD-1 decision model (FD-1, trained by the compounding loop) — decision + confidence + escalation — while the live /decide endpoint serves the FD-1, which may return a different label set (spam/ham); per-model measured performance is documented in our lab notes — ask us for the full writeup.

How to read this. Confidence is the model's self-reported certainty — 0.99 means “I'm sure”, 0.41 means “I'm guessing”. It is not a calibrated probability: well-calibrated on SMS-style inputs (ECE ≈ 0.05), less reliable on phishing-style inputs (ECE ≈ 0.21), and overconfident on sarcastic or adversarial text. Calls below the 0.49 line are escalated — in the pilot flow, a human reviewer decides, and the reviewer's answer becomes training data. That's the loop.

Live — call the real API

Send your own text to the live /decide endpoint and get a real decision back. It needs your API key (write to surety.ai@proton.me for access). First request after idle can take up to ~90 s — the page will tell you honestly instead of pretending it's broken.

Your key stays in this tab. It's sent only to the API, never stored. Text you submit is covered by our data-handling policy — never used for training without your written consent (full policy available on request).
