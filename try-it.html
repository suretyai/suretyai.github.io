What am I looking at, exactly?
Pre-computed outputs from the v3 sentiment model (ModernBERT + LoRA, trained by the compounding loop). The demo illustrates the mechanism — decision + confidence + escalation. The live /decide endpoint serves the production spam-v1 model, which may return a different label set (spam/ham). Per-model measured performance is in our lab notes — ask us for the full writeup.

Confidence is the model's self-reported certainty — 0.99 means “I'm sure”, 0.41 means “I'm guessing”. It is not a calibrated probability: well-calibrated on SMS-style inputs (ECE ≈ 0.05), less reliable on phishing-style inputs (ECE ≈ 0.21), and overconfident on sarcastic or adversarial text. Calls below the 0.49 line are escalated — in the pilot flow, a human reviewer decides, and the reviewer's answer becomes training data. That's the loop.
