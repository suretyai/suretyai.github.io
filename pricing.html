1 — What counts

One call, one decision.

Billing follows the API, not a pricing model you have to decode. Escalated decisions cost the same as confident ones — you pay for the call, not the outcome.

2 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

What counts as a decision?

One /decide call is one decision. The batch endpoint (/decide/batch) counts per text — 100 texts in one batch is 100 decisions. Health and version checks don’t count.

What happens when I hit a limit?

The API returns 429 with a Retry-After header — the same behavior you’d see in testing. On paid plans, we’ll reach out before your included volume runs dry so you can decide: upgrade, add overage, or wait for the next cycle. Nothing auto-charges.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Growth and Scale are month-to-month — cancel in one email and you’re done at the end of the billing cycle. No lock-in, no cancellation fees. Enterprise plans are annual by negotiation.

Do escalated decisions cost extra?

No. A decision that goes to human review costs the same as an automatic one. We don’t penalize you for the model being honest about uncertainty.

Why charge per decision instead of flat?

Because our pitch is that your review queue gets shorter every month as the loop learns. Per-decision pricing means the bill tracks the work we do for you — and shrinks if the loop does its job well.

Is the Sandbox really free?

Yes, but it’s genuinely limited: 10,000 decisions a month, one model version, community support, and the standard 100 req/min rate limit. It’s for evaluating and prototyping — production workloads belong on a paid plan.

The honest fine print.
  • These are planned rates while paid plans are still being built — early access is free, and there’s no SLA on any plan — the API runs on free-tier hosting, and it would be dishonest to sell guarantees on infrastructure we don’t control.
  • Willingness to pay per decision is still being validated with design partners; rates may move before launch. We’d rather adjust now than surprise you later.
  • No SOC 2, no ISO 27001, no independent pen test yet — see our security page for what we actually have.
