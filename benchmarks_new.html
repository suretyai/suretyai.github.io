Benchmarks

Measured in the lab. Published with the losses.

Every number here comes from a lab run on public data, with the method written down next to it. Where we're weak, we say so.

1 — WHAT WE MEASURE

Decision quality, not just accuracy.

Fronesa answers three kinds of questions: yes/no, multiple choice, and scored. We measure each on public benchmarks:

  • Accuracy — how often the top answer is right
  • Calibration (ECE) — whether 80% confidence means 80% right
  • Latency — p50 and p95 response time
