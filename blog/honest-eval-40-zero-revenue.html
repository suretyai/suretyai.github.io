Honest eval · Part 23

A scoreboard with zero revenue.

The company's internal scoreboard reads $0 revenue, 0 customers, 0 contracts. We keep it that way on purpose.

Fronesa engineering October 6, 2026 4 min read

The scoreboard

On the wall of this company — metaphorically; we are an online company, there is no wall — there is a scoreboard. It reads:

  • Revenue: $0
  • Customers: 0
  • Contracts: 0
  • Legal entity: none
  • Payment processor: none

And below that, a second column:

  • API test suite: 230/230 passing
  • SDK suite: 83/83 passing
  • v4 HELDOUT clean gate: passing (recall 0.5625, false positives 2, at the boundary)
  • Golden eval: passing
  • Dry-run labels published: 0
  • Human review verdicts: 0
  • Paired labels collected: 0

The first column is the one that matters. The second column is the one we work on. The discipline is in never letting the second column stand in for the first.

Why we keep it

Every internal document in this company carries the zero column or it carries a dishonesty. The learning loop can be real, the gates can pass, the dry run can grow from hundreds to thousands of seen items — and the company can still be worth $0, because worth is decided by someone paying. Until then, the second column is preparation, not progress.

What changes the first column

One customer. Not a pilot, not a design partner call — a contract, with money moving. Everything else on the second column exists to make that moment cheap to say yes to: the API that passes its suite, the SDK that installs clean, the gate that holds at the boundary, the ledger that records every human verdict from the first. Preparation is not nothing. It is just not revenue.

The first column is the one that matters. The second column is the one we work on.

The lesson

Vanity metrics are not a marketing problem; they are an internal one. The moment the team starts quoting the second column at itself, the company stops being able to read the first.

Chain of custody

Shift summaries and gate scripts in the company tree [LAB]; finance state per standing company records. No customer data exists; no production figures invented; no claims about future revenue.

Pre-publish blockers

  1. Legal spot-check of every number and every implied claim.
  2. Revenue/customer/entity figures re-verified at publish time — if any changes, the post changes or waits.
  3. Braa review on framing of the pre-entity period.
  4. No number in this post may be reused in marketing copy without its own spot-check.
