Fronesa — legal

Your data stays yours.

How pilot customer data is handled: encrypted in transit, isolated per pilot, never used for training without your written consent, deleted when you ask.

LAST UPDATED — OCTOBER 2026

1. Encryption & access

All API traffic travels over HTTPS (TLS). Stored pilot data lives on access-controlled infrastructure, reachable only by the small team operating the pilot — currently the founder and the systems running the service. No shared dashboards, no third-party analytics touching your texts.

2. Isolation per pilot

Each pilot customer’s data is kept separate: separate API keys, separate storage, separate model versions. Your texts never mingle with another customer’s, and one pilot’s data never improves another pilot’s model.

3. No training without written consent

This is the rule we hold ourselves to, in writing:

We do not train any model — shared or otherwise — on your data without your explicit written consent. The learning loop retrains your private version on your resolved escalations, because that’s the product you asked for. Nothing of yours flows into anyone else’s model, and nothing of yours is used to improve a shared base without you agreeing to it first, in writing.

If a pilot arrangement does include shared-model training, it will be spelled out in that pilot’s written agreement — never assumed.

4. Human review stays with you

Escalated decisions go to your own reviewers, not ours. We don’t see the content of your review queue unless the pilot arrangement explicitly says otherwise (for example, a joint debugging session you invite us to).

5. Retention & deletion

  • Pilot data is kept only for the duration of the pilot, plus a short wind-down window for final measurements — typically 30 days — unless your agreement says otherwise.
  • Deletion on request, any time: write to surety.ai@proton.me and we’ll delete your pilot data and confirm when it’s done.
  • At pilot end, data is deleted unless you explicitly ask us to keep it (for example, to resume later).

6. What we don’t claim

Plainly: we hold no security certifications (no SOC 2, no ISO 27001) and make no compliance-certification claims. What’s on this page is our operating practice, stated honestly — not an audited standard. If your procurement needs certifications we don’t have, tell us early and we’ll say so directly rather than improvising.
