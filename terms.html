Fronesa — legal

Pilot terms, in plain language.

Fronesa is in early pilot. These terms cover pilot access to the API and this site. If anything here is unclear, write to us — we'd rather explain than hide behind legalese.

LAST UPDATED — OCTOBER 2026

1. What this is

Fronesa is a decision API in early pilot. Access is granted for evaluation purposes so you can test whether it fits your use case. There is no service-level agreement: we do not promise any level of uptime, latency, or availability during the pilot.

The service is provided “as is,” without warranties of any kind — including accuracy, reliability, or fitness for a particular purpose.

2. Our numbers are lab measurements, not guarantees

Every benchmark figure on this site comes from synthetic-drift lab experiments on public data, documented in our lab notes. They are measured results from those runs — not promises about how the system will perform on your data, and not a guaranteed error rate.

How to read our claims: “measured” means we ran it and wrote down the number. It does not mean it will repeat on your traffic. A pilot exists precisely so you can measure it yourself, on your own data, before deciding anything.

3. Acceptable use

During the pilot, you agree not to:

  • Send illegal content through the API, or use it for any unlawful purpose.
  • Use decisions for high-stakes outcomes — medical diagnosis, legal judgments, credit or employment decisions, safety-critical systems — without meaningful human review of each decision that matters.
  • Attempt to disrupt, overload, or reverse-engineer the service beyond normal evaluation use.
  • Resell or redistribute pilot access to third parties without our written agreement.

If a use case sits in a gray area, ask us first: surety.ai@proton.me.

4. Your data

How we handle texts you send to the pilot API — retention, isolation, deletion, and our no-training-without-consent rule — is described on the data handling page, which forms part of these terms. Our privacy policy covers what we collect more broadly.

5. Ending pilot access

You can stop using the pilot at any time. We may suspend or end pilot access at any time — for example if the pilot program changes, or if these terms are violated. If that happens, we’ll tell you why when we reasonably can.

6. Liability, honestly limited

To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from pilot use — lost profits, lost data, or decisions you acted on. Our total liability for the pilot is limited to what you paid us for it, which during the free pilot is nothing. This is a free evaluation, and the liability cap reflects that.

7. Changes & contact

We may update these terms as the pilot evolves; we’ll note the date above when we do. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated terms.

Questions about these terms: surety.ai@proton.me — a human reads it.
