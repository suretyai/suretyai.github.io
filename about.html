About

A decision API that gets smarter from your reviewers.

Fronesa is a decision API for text. You send us text; we return a decision, a confidence score, and a flag for whether a human should look at it. The confident calls get automated. The uncertain ones get escalated to your reviewers. And every reviewer correction becomes training data — so the model improves on your data, week after week.

01 — What we believe

Four principles, no exceptions.

These aren't wall art. They decide what we ship, what we publish, and what we refuse.

i. Numbers, honestly measured.

Every performance figure we publish is traceable to a measured artifact — the model, the dataset split, the evaluation script. When we found numbers in our own drafts that we couldn't trace, we rebuilt them from the model rather than keep them. Our benchmarks show the losses alongside the wins. If we can't measure it, we don't claim it.

ii. Decisions that learn.

A frozen model is a depreciating asset — your data drifts, your policies evolve, and the model stays put. Fronesa closes the loop: reviewer corrections flow back into retraining, gated by evaluation, with versioning and rollback. We proved the machinery end-to-end before promising it. The loop is the product.

iii. Human judgment is the point.

We don't replace reviewers; we ration their attention. The easy calls get automated, the gray-area calls get escalated with a confidence score your people can audit. A moderation tool that hides its uncertainty is a liability. Ours tells you exactly when it isn't sure.

iv. Small, fast, auditable.

Our production model is measured in kilobytes and answers in milliseconds on a warm container (first request after ~15 idle minutes can take up to ~90 s on the free tier while it wakes). No GPU cluster, no black-box giant you can't inspect. Every decision carries a version, a confidence, and a request ID. Boring infrastructure is a feature — it's what lets you trust the answers.

02 — What we're not

Things we'll never be.

  • NOT — HYPE No inflated claims. No multiplier framing, no invented customers, no "revolutionary" anything. Our figures are lab measurements until real production traffic says otherwise, and we say so on the page.
  • NOT — A BLACK BOX No decisions without receipts. Every call returns its confidence, its model version, and a request ID. If we're wrong — especially confidently wrong — you can see it, count it, and correct it.
  • NOT — A REPLACEMENT FOR YOUR TEAM No "AI moderators." We do decision support for humans: automate the clear calls, escalate the hard ones. Anyone selling you the removal of human judgment from moderation is selling you their problem, not your solution.
  • NOT — CERTIFIED (YET) No badges we haven't earned. We have no SOC 2, no ISO 27001, no independent penetration test — and we say so plainly on our security page. We'll pursue them when the business justifies it, not before.
